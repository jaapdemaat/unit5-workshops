Unit 5: GCD Practices 03 — Overview

Duration: 15 weeks (Stage 2, weeks 1–15)  ·  Credits: 20  ·  Delivery: principally by Contexts tutors, runs in parallel with Unit 6

This unit has three core purposes:

  1. Students expand and consolidate the forms of writing introduced in Units 2 and 3, including descriptive, reflective, and critical writing
  2. Students are supported in developing a theoretical and contextual framework for the body of work they are producing in their Platforms in Unit 6
  3. Students investigate their practice position in relation to a range of ethical, cultural, critical and intellectual perspectives on both historical and contemporary practices

The connectivity

Unit 5 and Unit 6, which run concurrently, enable students to consider their practice in relation to cultural, social and theoretical contexts of contemporary Graphic Communication Design and discover emerging themes that begin to inform their creative practice. In Unit 5, students use writing to review, investigate and reflect on the development of their practice in relation to both the work they are producing in Unit 6 and debates in the larger context of Graphic Communication Design.

Assessment

The unit culminates in the production of a 2,500-word written and designed critical report. Assessment is holistic and summative, taking place at the end of the unit. Students receive formative feedback from tutors and peers throughout.

Session Logistics

Room setup (pre-arranged)

  • Two large tables, each with 15+ chairs
  • Flexible arrangement: tutors and students can rearrange based on that week's workshop activities and needs

Technology

  • Both facilitators and students bring laptops (required)
  • Most workshops are analogue (paper, Post-its, pens, marking)
  • Reflective writing tasks are digital: students write and hand in digitally

Session timing

  • All sessions start at 10:00 — no exceptions
  • Students instructed to arrive 15 minutes earlier (9:45)
  • Close the door at 10:00 to signal the session has started
  • Remind students to record attendance on Seats

Context

Unit 5 runs for 15 weeks. Each workshop is designed for two groups (15+ each) working in one classroom with two tutors. Most workshops use a shared intro + pre-task discussion, then parallel group work — but workshop structure varies based on pedagogical needs. Check the week-specific facilitator's note (on the main site) for that week's setup.

Co-teaching Model (flexible)

Shared moments

Both tutors, all 30+ students

  • Start of session: intro + frame
  • Pre-task discussion
  • Cross-group viewing or a comparable review moment
  • End of session: exit recap or synthesis + hand in

Parallel moments

Each tutor with their 15+ group

  • Reference introduction (conversational, not a lecture)
  • Main group work (students engage independently; tutor circulates, listens, asks genuine questions)
  • Sharing or viewing moments where groups show their work

Note: not all workshops will use all of these. The week-specific facilitator's note will confirm which moments apply that week.

Pre-session Alignment

Meet 15 min before — always agree on:

  • How you'll frame the week's purpose and connect it to Unit 6 / developing practice
  • What you'll listen for during group work
  • Exit recap or synthesis themes — how you'll weave activities back to the LOs
  • Logistics specific to that week's structure
  • Timing adjustments or pedagogical priorities for this workshop
